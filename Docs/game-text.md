### 欢迎新职工进厂　十二名同志今日报到

Plant Welcomes New Workers. Twelve Comrades Report Today

*从矿上调入的新同志分到各班组　宿舍已安排就绪*

*Newcomers transferred from the mines join their work teams. Dormitory rooms are ready.*

本报讯　今天上午，十二名新职工来厂报到。他们大多从矿上调来，有的在井下掘进队干了好几年。进厂前，大家在厂门口拉起横幅，合影留念。

Staff report. This morning twelve new workers reported to the plant. Most were transferred from the mines, and some spent years on underground tunneling crews. Before coming in, they hung a banner at the factory gate and posed for a group photo.

厂里已为新职工安排好宿舍，四人一间，床铺、桌子和炉子都已备齐。报到后，各车间派老师傅领着新同志熟悉厂区，认识洗选、运输和机修各个岗位。

The plant has arranged dormitory rooms for them, four to a room, with beds, desks and stoves all ready. After reporting in, veteran workers from each workshop showed the newcomers around the plant and introduced the washing, haulage and repair posts.

新职工先跟班学习，由老师傅带着熟悉设备和操作规程。从井下上来的同志，要重新适应地面上的倒班和交接班，有不明白的地方，随时向班长请教。

New workers will first learn on shift, with veteran workers guiding them through the equipment and operating rules. Comrades who have come up from underground must get used to surface shifts and handovers again. If anything is unclear, ask your team leader at any time.

厂工会提醒，宿舍取暖用煤可到煤场登记领取，生活上有困难，可向车间反映。欢迎新同志早日安下心来，和大家一起把生产搞好。

The plant union reminds new workers that heating coal for the dormitories can be signed for at the coal yard, and any hardships in daily life can be raised with the workshop. We hope our new comrades soon settle in and join everyone in keeping production strong.

### 索道检修完成　恢复日常运转

Cableway Maintenance Complete, Daily Runs Resume

本报讯　运输班近日完成索道例行检修，对吊斗、托轮及装卸设备逐项检查，清理落煤，为转动部位补加润滑油。需要继续观察的情况，已记入交接簿。

Staff report. The haulage crew recently finished routine maintenance on the cableway, checking the buckets, rollers and loading gear one by one, clearing spilled coal and greasing the moving parts. Anything that needs further watching has been entered in the handover log.

恢复运行前，班组沿线复查，随后进行吊斗试车。值班人员留在装卸两端，核对吊斗进出站情况。试车正常后，运输班已按原有安排恢复日常运煤。

Before restarting, the crew walked the line for a second check, then ran trial buckets. Staff on duty at the loading and unloading ends checked the buckets in and out of each station. With the trial run going smoothly, the haulage crew has returned to hauling coal on the usual schedule.

## 开场独白

天空画面中央

1. After Grandpa’s funeral, I went back to his bedroom to sort through the things he had left behind.
2. Among his belongings, I found a photograph, yellow with age. Grandpa and his coworkers stood together outside the factory gate.
3. Looking at it, the stories Grandpa told me when I was little came rushing back, one after another.
4. I found his old diary too. I had always wanted to visit Mom and Grandpa’s hometown, and this was my chance.
5. With the diary and photograph packed away, I rode my motorcycle to the coal washing plant where he used to work.
6. The engine is quiet now. Standing outside the factory, I wonder if the gate still looks the way it did in that photograph.

## 外公日记

### 第一页　First Day at the Plant

左半页

**October 3, 1980**

Five years of drilling blast holes underground with Tunneling Team 4. Today I was transferred to the coal washing plant at the foot of the mountain.

Twelve of us started at the plant today. Before we went in, they lined us up at the gate under a red banner for a photo.

I was given Room 207 in the workers' dormitory, second floor, the second door past the stairs. Four beds, one table, one stove. From the window I can see the cableway climbing the mountain.

A new job deserves a new diary. This is the first page.

右半页

**October 14, 1980**

Zhao Jianguo has the bed across from mine. Five years underground, like me. In the photo at the gate he stood right beside me.

On the night shift the old hands told us a story. Years ago, a worker slipped out by the north mountain road after his shift and climbed all the way to the ridge. The gate guard wrote him up. He said it was worth it. From up there you could see every light in the plant at once.

Jianguo has not stopped talking about it. Tonight he stood at the window a long time. I looked too. In the dark I could only make out the cableway, climbing toward the ridge.

### 第二页　Room 207

左半页

**November 9, 1980**

Training is over. I am in the washing shop now. Jianguo was put on the cableway crew at the lower station, so our shifts no longer line up.

Tonight in 207 he brought up the ridge again. He has asked the old hands about it more than once.

So we made a deal. The first night we are both off, we go up to the ridge together and see the lights for ourselves.

He shook my hand on it, as if it were official.

右半页

**December 4, 1980**

Winter came early. We signed for heating coal at the coal yard and lit the stove in 207. Now the four of us sit around it before the night shift, gloves drying on the stovepipe.

Jianguo says the north road is no good in the dark. The empty buckets go back up the line all night, so we will ride one. Tonight he drew the way on the back of an old work ticket. Down the main road to the lower station, every tower up the line, and a cross at the top for the ridge.

He put it in the table drawer. He says it stays there until the night we go.

### 第三页　Next Time

左半页

**December 26, 1980**

My girl is one year old today.

It was also the first night Jianguo and I were both off. Before I went home this morning, I told him I might not make it back by dark. He said go. The lights will still be there.

I did not make it back. She still will not let anyone but her mother hold her. My wife has not slept a full night in months. I could not leave her with all of it.

Next time, then.

右半页

**July 18, 1983**

I have not written in this book for a long time.

Jianguo and I were both off on Saturday night. We had it all planned this time. But it had rained for three days by then, and the water coming down the north gully washed out the footing of the fifth tower. The cableway was stopped.

They say it will run again after the rains.

Jianguo said once it runs, then. He took the map out of the drawer, looked at it a while, and put it back.

### 第四页　The Cableway Stops

左半页

**September 2, 1986**

My girl started school yesterday. Her mother walked her there, with a satchel nearly as big as she is.

Jianguo is on nights now and I am on days. Most weeks we only see each other at the gate at the evening changeover, him going in, me coming out. Sometimes he says, the ridge. I say, next time we are both off.

The other two beds in 207 have been empty since spring. No new hands have come to the plant in a long time.

右半页

**April 12, 1988**

Word came this morning. The plant is stopping, and no one can say when it will start again.

At noon the cableway stopped. The buckets just hung there on the line, all the way up toward the ridge. Nobody in the washing shop said much. We cleaned the machines anyway.

Jianguo and I sat up late in 207 tonight.

From the window I can still see the cableway climbing the mountain. Nothing on it moves.
